---
name: Semantic state meaning
description: Why Career Scout distinguishes uncertainty, reversible hiding, and destructive states visually.
---

Use a restrained palette with consistent meaning rather than a different badge color for each job source or metadata field. Personalization adjustments and search-stage scores are informational, not warnings.

**Why:** The user asked to reduce competing badge treatments and establish success, warning, hidden, destructive, and neutral states while keeping the app supportive rather than judgmental.

**How to apply:** Confirmed success is positive; uncertain availability and reanalysis notices need attention; suppressed sources and reversibly closed jobs are hidden, not errors. A report that a job is closed remains uncertain rather than a confirmed destructive event. Reserve destructive emphasis for concrete removals and failures. Keep labels explicit, and confirm saves or job actions only after actual success.
