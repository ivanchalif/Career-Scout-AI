---
name: Job score communication
description: User-facing language and visual treatment for search match and profile fit.
---

Explain Search match as the lightweight online-discovery stage and Profile fit as the deeper analysis of the posting against the candidate profile. Do not imply their numbers share a methodology or that one converts into the other.

**Why:** The user says unexplained scales and strong red styling for low scores feel judgmental. They want the stages explained with an expandable breakdown and plain-language Strong fit, Possible fit, and Stretch role labels.

**How to apply:** Keep lower fit visually calm and reserve red for concrete dealbreakers or errors. Surface whether the deeper score is pending, current, or needs reanalysis. Explain score evidence without inventing sub-scores that the model does not produce.
